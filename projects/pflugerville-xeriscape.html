The Challenge · Pflugerville

Dead turf. Expansive clay.

14 Native Species · Zero Irrigation

A treeless suburban lot in Pflugerville with 2,800 square feet of dead St. Augustine grass baking on expansive clay soil. No shade canopy. No drainage plan. No irrigation system worth saving. The homeowner wanted zero lawn maintenance and lower water bills — permanently.

The brief: strip every square inch of dead turf, re-grade the entire lot for proper surface drainage away from the foundation, and replace it all with a curated native Texas xeriscape that requires zero permanent irrigation, zero chemical fertilizers, and zero weekly mowing. Every bed transition defined by steel. Every pathway crushed stone.

Where most contractors would lay fresh sod over the same failing clay and call it a day, Red Agave removed every blade, corrected the grade, and installed a 14-species native botanical palette designed to thrive on Central Texas rainfall alone. Decomposed granite pathways weave through boulder groupings and specimen agaves. COB uplights bring the entire composition to life after dark.

Native xeriscape. Not sod.

System Details

Native Botanical Palette

  • —Whale’s Tongue Agave (Agave ovatifolia), Blue Glow Agave, Artichoke Agave (Agave parryi)
  • —Flame Acanthus, Mexican Honeysuckle, Turk’s Cap, Purple Coneflower
  • —Autumn Sage, Mexican Bush Sage, Blackfoot Daisy
  • —Mexican Feathergrass, Gulf Muhly, Lindheimer Muhly, Yucca rostrata

Steel Edging System

  • —340 linear feet of 1/4-inch mild steel edging ($6/lf)
  • —Defines every bed-to-pathway transition
  • —Natural rust patina — zero maintenance finish
  • —Clean geometric lines separating granite from planting beds

Decomposed Granite Hardscape

  • —18 tons decomposed granite (Texas Tan) at $55/ton
  • —Pathways weave through boulder groupings and specimen plantings
  • —8 hand-set limestone boulders (200–400 lb each)
  • —Permeable surface — zero stormwater runoff concerns

COB Accent Lighting

  • —12 COB uplights (CRI 90+, 2700K warm-white)
  • —Independent zones for specimen plant illumination
  • —ESP32 micro-controller running WLED firmware
  • —App-based scene control — warm ambient via smartphone
